    Connect NotebookLM by hand

    NotebookLM reads the papers. This tool holds the criteria, writes the prompts, reads the answers and does the scoring. Do this once per notebook, and again whenever you lock a new methodology version.

    1. Open notebooklm.google.com with your Google account and create a notebook, e.g. one per review.
    2. Save the source pack for each study type you will assess and add it to the notebook as a source. Use Upload for the file, or Copied text with the copy button. The pack holds every SciRAP criterion, the official guidance and your locked methodology.
    3. Upload the study PDFs as sources, up to 5 papers per run.
    4. In Assess papers, add each paper and copy its prompt. In NotebookLM's source panel, tick only that paper and the source pack, then paste the prompt into the chat.
    5. Copy NotebookLM's whole answer (the JSON block), paste it back into the paper's box and press Read answer.

    Scoring rules

    Reporting and methodological quality are calculated exactly as in the official SciRAP Excel sheets. SciRAP does not define a relevance score or an overall rating, so those two are your rules, set below and saved with every export.

    From the SciRAP sheets (fixed)

    • Points: fulfilled 1, partially fulfilled 0.5, not fulfilled 0, not reported 0 (method only).
    • % fulfilled = points ÷ (criteria in section − criteria marked REMOVE) × 100, per section and per subsection.
    • Free-text items (in vitro R24/M16, in vivo R31/M18) are comments and are not scored.
    • Reporting criteria have no "not reported" option in the sheet. If NotebookLM says "not reported", it is recorded as not fulfilled.
    • A criterion left unanswered counts as 0, as a blank cell does in the sheet. The paper is flagged as incomplete.

    Inclusion decision (your thresholds)

    • Relevance % = (directly relevant × 1 + indirectly relevant × 0.5) ÷ number of relevance items × 100, using the selection values listed in the SciRAP sheet.
    • Quality average = (reporting % + methodology %) ÷ 2.
    • Include when relevance % ≥ the relevance threshold and quality average ≥ the quality threshold. Otherwise Exclude, with the reason shown.

    Thresholds are set per run, before any paper is assessed, in Assess papers.
