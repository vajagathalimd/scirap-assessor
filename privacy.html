Using the tool as a guest

As a guest, nothing you enter is sent to our database. Your work exists only in your open browser tab and is gone when you close or reload it, unless you download it yourself as a JSON file.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and the tool has no tracking or analytics.

Legal basis

[[To be confirmed with the data protection officer, e.g. your consent when creating an account (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)), or the performance of a task in the public interest for university research tools (art. 6(1)(e))]].

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in, in the [[EU region, e.g. Stockholm / Frankfurt]] region, as our data processor.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google", under Google's own privacy policy.
  • The page loads its open-source program libraries from this website. [[If you did not set up the lib folder: from the jsDelivr and cdnjs content networks, which see your IP address when they deliver those files.]]
  • NotebookLM: the tool never sends your papers anywhere. You copy prompts into NotebookLM yourself, under Google's terms.

Administrators of the tool see a list of accounts: email address, sign-in method, account dates, and how many methodologies and papers each account has saved. They can block or delete accounts, and every such action is logged. The administrator view does not show the content of your work. The organiser can technically access stored content through the database administration console, and does so only to run and support the service or when you ask for help.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. [[Optional: accounts with no sign-in for 24 months are deleted after an email warning.]]

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Account → Download all my data gives you everything as a JSON file.
  • Erasure: Account → Delete my account and data deletes your account and all saved work immediately.
  • Rectification: edit your work in the tool, or contact us.
  • You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se).
